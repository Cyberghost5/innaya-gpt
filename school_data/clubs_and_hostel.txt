INNAYATULLAH INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL, IWO, OSUN STATE
STUDENT SOCIETIES, SPORTS CLUBS & BOARDING HOSTEL GUIDELINES
================================================================================

1. CO-CURRICULAR CLUBS & STUDENT SOCIETIES:
- Robotics & AI Innovation Guild: Meets Tuesdays & Thursdays (3:30 PM) in STEM Lab 2. Lead Advisor: Engr. David Chukwu. Focus: Arduino hardware, line-follower robots, 3D printing.
- Literary & Debate Society: Meets Wednesdays (3:45 PM) in the College Amphitheater. Master Trainer: Mrs. Victoria Danjuma. Prepares students for National British Parliamentary Interschool Debates.
- Junior Engineers, Technicians and Scientists (JETS Club): Meets Mondays (3:30 PM) in the Chemistry Lab. Experiments with solar ovens and biofuel cells.
- Model United Nations (IIS-MUN): Meets Fridays (3:30 PM) in Senior Hall. Focus: International geopolitics and public diplomacy.
- IIS Sports & Football Academy: Daily training (4:45 PM - 6:00 PM) on the main school pitch and basketball courts under Coach Babatunde Adele.

2. BOARDING HOUSE SCHEDULE & HOSTEL TIMETABLE:
- 05:30 AM: Fajr / Morning Prayer & Personal Hygiene.
- 06:15 AM: Morning Dormitory Inspection by Housemasters.
- 07:00 AM: Breakfast at the Scholars' Dining Hall.
- 07:30 AM: Movement to Academic Classrooms.
- 04:00 PM: Return to Hostels, Sports, and Leisure.
- 06:30 PM: Evening Dinner at Dining Hall.
- 07:45 PM - 09:15 PM: Compulsory Night Study Prep in Classrooms.
- 09:30 PM: Evening Roll Call & Dormitory Devotion.
- 10:00 PM: Lights Out (Strict Silence in all Hostels).
