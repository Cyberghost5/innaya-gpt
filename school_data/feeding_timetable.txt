================================================================================
INNAYATULLAH INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL, IWO, OSUN STATE
SCHOLARS' DINING HALL - DAILY FEEDING TIMETABLE & MEAL SCHEDULE
================================================================================

1. GENERAL DINING HOURS:
- Morning Breakfast (Boarders & Early Day Students): 07:00 AM - 07:40 AM
- Mid-Morning Snack & Fruit Break (All Students): 10:15 AM - 10:45 AM
- Senior School Main Lunch Break (SS1 - SS3): 12:45 PM - 01:30 PM
- Junior School Main Lunch Break (JSS1 - JSS3): 01:30 PM - 02:15 PM
- Boarders' Evening Dinner: 06:30 PM - 07:45 PM
- Boarders' Light Night Beverage: 09:15 PM - 09:30 PM

2. WEEKLY LUNCH MENU (SENIOR SECONDARY STUDENTS):

* MONDAY:
  - Main Dish: Hot Nigerian Jollof Rice with Golden Fried Plantain (Dodo).
  - Protein: Herb-Roasted Chicken or Crispy Fried Fish.
  - Sides: Steamed Garden Veggies and chilled Hibiscus-Zobo Juice.
  - Vegetarian / Special Option: Vegetable Jollof Rice with Baked Tofu and steamed corn.

* TUESDAY:
  - Main Dish: Chicken & Vegetable Pasta Stir-fry or Tomato Basil Spaghetti.
  - Protein: Grilled Chicken Drumstick or Boiled Egg.
  - Sides: Fresh Coleslaw and Chilled Sweet Melon slices.
  - Vegetarian Option: Stir-fry Pasta with mushrooms, carrots, and sweet peas.

* WEDNESDAY (TRADITIONAL HERITAGE MEAL):
  - Main Dish: Pounded Yam or Semovita.
  - Soup: Rich Egusi Soup with Bitterleaf or Fresh Vegetable Efo Riro.
  - Protein: Tender Stewed Beef, Goat Meat, or Fresh Catfish.
  - Vegetarian Option: Efo Riro with Sautéed Mushrooms and Moi-Moi (steamed bean loaf).

* THURSDAY:
  - Main Dish: Egg and Sweet Corn Fried Rice.
  - Protein: Peppered Crispy Chicken or Glazed Beef Strips.
  - Sides: Vegetable Spring Roll and Fresh Orange slices.
  - Vegetarian Option: Vegetable Fried Rice with Pan-seared Tofu.

* FRIDAY:
  - Main Dish: Spiced Basmati Rice or Roasted Yam & Plantain Porridge.
  - Protein: Smoked Tilapia or Grilled Herb Chicken.
  - Sides: Mixed Green Salad and Vanilla/Strawberry Cup Dessert.

3. CAFETERIA RULES & HYGIENE PROTOCOLS:
- Students must present their RFID Smart Card at the entry turnstile.
- Hands must be sanitized at the automatic wash stations prior to entering.
- Waste sorting is mandatory: Compostable food scraps, recyclable plastic bottles, and paper napkins.
